# Full-Stack Agent Master Plan

## Phase 0 – Research & Validation
- **Lead:** Research Agent
- **Status:** ✅ Report delivered 2026-02-22 (conditional GO)
- **Outputs:** Domain overview, spread evidence, risk log, required roles/tools
- **Next:** Build budget scenarios, confirm specialist staffing (compliance, assay, logistics)

## Phase 1 – Finance & Staffing Blueprint *(in progress)*
- Define capital requirements (Pilot vs. Full-scale)
- Map investor-backed models (external capital takes risk; we provide tech/ops)
- Identify required hires/partners (domain, compliance, assay)
- Deliverable: `FINANCE_PLAN.md` + partner shortlist

## Phase 2 – Data & Engine Build
- **Engine Agent**
  - Ingest LBMA, MCX, SGE, customs tariff feeds
  - Build spread calculator + custody tracker + hedging hooks
  - Extend DB schema, APIs, dashboard views
- **Ops Agent**
  - Monitoring, cron jobs, reporting automation

## Phase 3 – Go-to-Market & Investor Packaging
- **Growth Agent**
  - Target customer & investor collateral (decks, product brief, compliance onboarding)
  - Outreach scripts, limited pilot partnerships
- **Ops Agent**
  - SOPs for customs/duty, logistics workflows, AML/KYC runbooks

## Phase 4 – Execution & Learning Loop
- Launch pilot with external capital partners
- Profit Validator Agent checks post-launch metrics
- Ideation Agent continues to feed new opportunities; Profit Validator gates them
- Update playbooks and automation based on retrospectives

---

## Agent Framework
1. **Ideation Agent** → surfaces new opportunities from market signals
2. **Research Agent** → deep validation & risk mapping
3. **Profit Validator Agent** → financial viability & GO/NO-GO
4. **Engine Agent** → builds product
5. **Growth Agent** → GTM + revenue
6. **Ops Agent** → execution, monitoring, compliance

All new ideas flow through Ideation → Research → Profit Validator before entering build.
